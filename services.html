Process

From first call to launch day

A predictable rhythm so you can plan around it. You'll always know which stage we're in and what I need from you next.

Week 0 — Discovery

A paid discovery session. You leave with a written scope, a fixed quote and a delivery date, whether or not we work together.

Weeks 1–2 — Design

Wireframes, then high-fidelity screens in Figma. Two rounds of revisions included.

Weeks 3+ — Build

Development in two-week sprints with a demo at the end of each. Feedback lands in the next sprint, never "later".

Launch — Handover

Deployment, analytics, a recorded walkthrough and 30 days of support included.

What you get every week

  • A working staging link you can share internally
  • A short written update — wins, blockers, next steps
  • Access to the issue board and repository
  • A 30-minute call if you'd rather talk it through

Working with other teams

I slot into existing teams comfortably — Figma files, Jira, Slack and your own git workflow. I've also worked alongside in-house designers and backend engineers without stepping on toes.

FAQ

Questions I get asked a lot

How soon can you start?
I usually have capacity within two to three weeks. If you have a hard deadline, mention it in your enquiry and I'll tell you honestly whether it's achievable.
Do you work with existing codebases?
Yes — roughly half my work is improving something that already exists. I'll audit first, then recommend the smallest change that solves the problem rather than a rewrite by default.
What do you need from me to quote?
A sentence about the goal, any designs or links you already have, and your rough deadline. That's enough for an initial estimate within one business day.
Can you work with our in-house designers?
Absolutely, and it often produces the best results. I'm comfortable working directly in Figma, commenting on designs early, and flagging accessibility or performance issues before they reach code.
What happens after launch?
Every build includes 30 days of support. After that, most clients move to a care plan — but there's no obligation, and you'll have full documentation either way.

Not sure which service fits?

Send over a link and a sentence about the goal. I'll reply with an honest recommendation — even when that recommendation is "you don't need me for this".
