How we work

A process without the mystery

You always know what's happening, what's next, and what it costs. No black boxes, no surprise invoices.

01 — Discovery

A 60-minute call and a written brief. We agree on scope, success metrics and what we are deliberately not building.

02 — Design & prototype

Wireframes and a clickable prototype so you can react to something real before a line of production code is written.

03 — Build in the open

Weekly demos on a staging URL, tickets in a board you can see, and a Slack channel for the small questions.

04 — Launch & handover

Deployment, monitoring, documentation and a recorded walkthrough so your team owns it with confidence.
