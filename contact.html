Before you write

Quick answers

I'm not sure what I need yet — is that okay?
Completely. Plenty of projects start as a vague idea. Describe the problem in your own words and I'll help you work out whether you need a new build, a fix, or nothing at all.
Do you sign NDAs?
Yes, happy to. Send yours over with the enquiry, or I can provide a standard mutual NDA.
How do payments work?
Projects are split into milestone payments — typically 40% to start, 40% at build sign-off and 20% on launch. Care plans are billed monthly in advance.
Do you work with clients outside the UK?
Regularly. I've worked with teams across Europe, North America and Australia, and I keep at least a two-hour overlap with every client's working day.
